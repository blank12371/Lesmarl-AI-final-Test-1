Privacy

Lesmarl AI does not track users with third-party analytics or hidden scripts. The app keeps chat data in the browser's local storage when possible and does not collect internal logs containing prompts or API keys.

When using free OpenRouter models, prompts may be processed by third-party model providers. Do not enter passwords, personal data, secret keys, or other sensitive information.

Messages are sent to the server only for the purpose of model generation and are not used for advertising or profiling. This is a self-hosted product and may be modified to match your deployment policies.

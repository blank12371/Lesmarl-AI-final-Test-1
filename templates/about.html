About Lesmarl AI

Made by Mashrur Ahmed Arib. Lesmarl AI is a polished AI chat application designed to feel like a modern product, built with Flask, OpenRouter, and hand-authored HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

The goal is to make AI assistance feel thoughtful and useful without sacrificing clarity, speed, or trust. The app is designed for free and low-cost models, resilient fallback handling, safe rendering of content, and a clean user experience.

AI output can be inaccurate or incomplete. It should be reviewed before acting on it. The software is provided as is, without warranty or guarantee of accuracy, availability, or fitness for a particular purpose.
